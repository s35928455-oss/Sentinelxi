⚠️ Benchmark Limitations & Operational Constraints

Dataset Synthesis: Evaluation figures are computed using labeled historical benchmark telemetry and synthetic adversarial injection traces. Operational SOC environments may present novel zero-day behaviors, custom obfuscated payloads, or atypical distributed botnet dynamics not fully captured in synthetic tests.

Human-in-the-Loop Requirement: SentinelX AI outputs are engineered as decision-support telemetry. Automated containment actions (such as firewall IP quarantining) must adhere to organizational change-control policies and undergo review by security analysts.

🛡️ About SentinelX AI Platform

SentinelX AI is an intelligent cybersecurity incident analysis, correlation, and response platform designed to eliminate SOC fatigue and accelerate mean-time-to-respond (MTTR).

Combining deterministic rule-based heuristic correlation with autonomous generative AI reasoning, SentinelX transforms thousands of raw security logs into clustered, actionable incident dossiers with evidence graphs and safe playbooks.

🔍 Core Detection Methodology

01
Streaming Ingestion & Normalization

Parses heterogeneous CSV and JSON logs into unified schemas conforming to security event taxonomies.

02
Sliding-Window Heuristic Correlation

Evaluates temporal thresholds, IP velocity, and behavioral deviation across time slices.

03
Autonomous AI Synthesis & Triage

Generates human-readable incident narratives, assesses false positive likelihood, and provides defensive next steps.

Detection Engine Sliding Windows & MITRE Heuristics

⚖️ Responsible AI & Ethical Disclaimers

Safe Defensive Operations: SentinelX AI does not perform intrusive exploitation or offensive penetration testing. All capabilities are strictly defensive, focusing on detection, containment verification, and SOC efficiency.

Data Privacy: Ingested log streams remain isolated in memory and local secure storage. No sensitive user credentials or plaintext passwords should ever be logged in cleartext.
